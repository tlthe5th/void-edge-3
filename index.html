    📖 Dual-Mode Field Manual

    MODE A: VOID EDGE CHRONICLES 3.0
    • 10-Shot Match: Each team drafts 5 escalating marbles (1pt, 2pt, 3pt, 5pt, 10pt) from their exterior tray.
    • Gravity Funnel & Touch Rule: Knock enemy marbles into the central Void pit to score their points. Dropping your own untouched marble in awards 0 points. Tied after 10 shots? Sudden Death 10pt Diamonds!
    MODE B: OFFICIAL BUMPER POOL RULES
    • Simultaneous Opening Bank Break: Both players bank their marked cue ball off a side rail toward their target pocket on the count of 3. Whoever sinks their ball—or stops closest to their target pocket—keeps the board as-is and shoots next with their next ball in line!
    • Tie Shootout (Both Sink Simultaneously): If both players sink their ball at the same time on the count of 3, each spots their next ball in line (starting to the right of the opponent's goal) and shoots simultaneously on 3 again until one misses!
    • Bumper Caroms & Penalties: Use the 12 rubber bumper posts and rails to bank around blockers. Sinking another ball before your marked ball, or dropping your own ball in the wrong cup, awards your opponent 2 free pocketed balls!
