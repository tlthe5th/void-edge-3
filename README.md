## Overview
A digital tabletop arena combining geometry, gravity, and tactics. Players launch marbles across a tiered board toward a central pitch-black void, defending high-value pieces while attempting to knock opponent assets off the edge.

## Core Rules & Setup
* **Board & Equipment:** A square upper tier sloping into a central void hole, manipulated via opposing cue sticks for 2-player local or CPU matches.
* **Marble Point Values:**
  * **White (Scout):** 1 Point
  * **Yellow (Skirmisher):** 2 Points
  * **Red (Vanguard):** 3 Points
  * **Green (Enforcer):** 5 Points
  * **Blue / Cyan (Heavy Diamond):** 10 Points
* **Opening Coin Toss:** The winner chooses to **Attack First** (corner gambit) or **Defer** for the final anchor shot.
* **Scoring & Mechanics:** 10 alternating moves per match. Knocking an opponent's marble into the void scores points; self-destructs yield zero; ties trigger a Sudden Death Rim Shootout with 10-point marbles.
